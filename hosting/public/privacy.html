The short version

KickOffCal does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or contacts. It stores what you follow and the calendar events it created, on your device and against an anonymous identifier, so it can keep your fixtures up to date. Purchases go through Apple or Google. Analytics and advertising run only with your consent where the law requires it.

1. Who we are

KickOffCal is operated by [operator name and address — to confirm]. Contact: [contact address — to confirm].

2. What the app stores and why

  • An anonymous identifier. On first launch the app creates a random identifier with Firebase Authentication. It carries no personal details and is how the service knows which fixtures a device wants updates for.
  • What you follow. The teams, competitions, athletes and series you follow, and your preferences (reminder times, calendar colour, event style), are stored on your device. The list of followed items is also stored against your anonymous identifier so schedule changes can reach your device.
  • Push token. A device push token (Apple or Google) is stored with your anonymous identifier so the service can wake the app silently when a fixture changes. It is not used to send marketing.
  • Calendar events. With your permission the app writes events to your device calendar, or to a Google Calendar you connect. A record of the events it created stays on your device so it can update or remove only its own entries. If you connect Google Calendar, the app requests access limited to calendars it creates, and the authorisation is held on your device.
  • Purchases. Premium subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Our billing provider, RevenueCat, receives the purchase receipt and links it to your anonymous identifier so your subscription can be restored on a new device. We do not see your payment details.
  • Diagnostics. Anonymous records of the app's sync runs and of fixture sources are kept to detect broken data feeds. They contain no personal data.

3. Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to measure how features are used, for example how many people follow a team or start a trial. Where your region requires consent, analytics runs only after you agree in the app's consent notice, and you can change your choice in the app at any time. Analytics data is associated with an app-instance identifier, not with your name.

4. Advertising

The free version may show banner advertising through Google AdMob. On iPhone, advertising is non-personalised and the app does not request permission to track you. On Android, advertising is personalised only where you consent in the app's consent notice; otherwise it is non-personalised. Premium removes advertising.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not collect contacts, location, photos or your calendar's other events. Team crests and photographs shown in the app are fetched from public sources by your device; those requests carry your device's network address like any web request.

6. Where data is processed

The service runs on Google Firebase in the United States. Transfers from the UK and EU rely on Google's standard contractual protections. [Confirm region and wording.]

7. Retention

Data linked to your anonymous identifier is kept while you use the app. Deleting your data from the app (Preferences, Data and privacy, Delete my data) removes the followed-items record, push token and entitlement mirror from our servers and resets the identifier. Purchase records are retained by the stores and our billing provider under their policies. Diagnostic records are aggregated and expire on a rolling basis.

8. Your rights

You can delete your data from within the app at any time. A web form for deletion requests from people who no longer have the app will be available at kickoffcal.app. You can also contact us at [contact address] to exercise rights you have under data protection law, including access and erasure.

9. Children

KickOffCal is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We will post updates to this policy here and note the date above. Material changes will be flagged in the app.
